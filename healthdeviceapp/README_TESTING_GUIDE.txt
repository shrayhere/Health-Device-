HOW TO RUN:
1. First time only:
   Right-click "Setup_Prerequisites.bat" and run it to ensure OpenCV & MediaPipe are installed.
   (Requires Python 3.10+ installed with "Add Python to PATH").

2. Launch App:
   Double-click "Health Device.exe".

3. Test AI Posture Tracker:
   - On the Home screen, click "Launch" or navigate to "AI Posture Tracker".
   - Click "Turn ON" to start tracking.
   - The AI will calibrate your posture, monitor your spine, and give soft voice reminders.
   - You can close the camera preview window anytime (the AI keeps tracking in the background).
   - Click "Show Preview" in the app if you want to bring the camera window back.
   - Step away from the camera to test the "Person Not Visible" detection.
   - Bending your back will immediately drop the score and alert you.

Enjoy testing!
